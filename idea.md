# Email Signature Manager — Product Plan

*Working name: FooterPilot*

---

## 1. The Idea in One Line

A simple, affordable tool that lets a company design one professional email signature and automatically apply it to every employee's email — with marketing banners and click tracking built in.

---

## 2. The Problem

Most companies have messy, inconsistent email signatures:

- **Every employee makes their own.** Different fonts, old logos, wrong titles, missing phone numbers, personal quotes.
- **Rebrands never fully land.** When a company changes its logo or colors, half the staff keep the old signature for months.
- **IT and HR waste time.** Someone has to email instructions to every new hire and chase people to update their signatures.
- **A free marketing channel goes unused.** Every company sends thousands of emails a day, but almost none use that space to promote a webinar, offer or announcement.
- **Existing tools are built for large enterprises.** Current market leaders are powerful but expensive, complex to set up and feel dated for small and mid-sized teams.

---

## 3. Our Solution

One central place where an admin designs the company signature once, and it appears on every employee's email automatically — no action needed from employees.

**Why it wins:**

- **Simple:** set up in minutes, not days.
- **Affordable:** fair per-user pricing, with a free tier for small teams.
- **Hands-off:** new hires and title changes update on their own.
- **Marketing-ready:** banners and click tracking turn every email into a promotion opportunity.
- **Private by design:** we never read or touch anyone's emails — we only manage the signature.

---

## 4. Who It's For

**Primary buyers:** companies with 20–300 employees, anywhere in the world.

**Who signs up and who benefits:**

- **IT admin / office manager:** wants consistency and zero manual work.
- **Marketing team:** wants brand consistency and a free promotional channel.
- **Founders / leadership:** want the company to look professional.

**Industries that care most:** real estate, recruitment, law firms, schools and education, agencies, consulting and funded startups.

**Partners (later):** IT service providers (MSPs) that manage email for many small businesses.

---

## 5. Version 1 (V1) — Launch Product

**Goal:** prove companies will pay. Focus only on Google Workspace (Gmail) users and keep the product simple and polished.

### 5.1 Free Signature Generator (public website)

A free tool where anyone can create a personal email signature without signing up.

**Why:** brings in steady search traffic from people looking for "email signature generator," builds brand awareness and lets us invite visitors to manage signatures for their whole team.

### 5.2 Company Sign-Up & Guided Setup

The admin signs up with their Google Workspace account and approves our app once for the whole company, guided by a simple step-by-step walkthrough (with screenshots and a short video). Optional live setup help is available for non-technical teams.

**Why:** setup is the biggest point where customers drop off. Making it feel easy and safe is critical to winning trust.

### 5.3 Automatic Employee Sync

The app automatically pulls in every employee's name, job title, phone number and photo from the company's existing directory.

**Why:** nobody has to type in staff details, and the data is always accurate.

### 5.4 Signature Designer

A visual, drag-and-drop editor to build the company signature: logo, colors, fonts, contact details, social media links and a company tagline. Includes a small set of ready-made professional templates to start from.

**Why:** admins are not designers. Templates and a visual editor let anyone create a professional result in minutes.

### 5.5 Live Preview

See exactly how the signature will look for different employees before applying it.

**Why:** builds confidence and avoids embarrassing mistakes going out to every employee.

### 5.6 One-Click Apply to Everyone

Apply the signature to every employee's email at once. Employees don't need to do anything.

**Why:** this is the core promise of the product — company-wide consistency in one click.

### 5.7 Automatic Updates

New hires automatically get the signature. Title, phone or name changes update automatically. Design changes roll out to everyone instantly.

**Why:** removes ongoing manual work — the main reason companies keep paying month after month.

### 5.8 Promotional Banners

Add a clickable banner to everyone's signature (for example, "Join our webinar Friday" or "Festive sale is live") and change it anytime.

**Why:** turns the signature into a marketing channel and brings the marketing team in as a second champion for the product.

### 5.9 Basic Click Tracking

See how many people clicked on banners and links.

**Why:** proves value to marketing and gives customers a reason to stay subscribed.

### 5.10 Pricing & Billing

- **Free tier:** for very small teams, with our small "Powered by" link in the signature.
- **Paid plan:** per user, per month, with the "Powered by" link removed.
- **Simple self-serve checkout** with international payments.

**Why:** the free tier spreads the product by word of mouth; per-user pricing grows naturally as customers hire.

### 5.11 Trust & Privacy Basics

A clear privacy policy, terms of service and a plain-language page explaining what we can and cannot access ("We never read your emails").

**Why:** admins are approving company-wide access, so trust is a key part of the sale, not an extra.

### V1 Success Milestone

- First **5 paying companies**
- First **10 customer reviews** on software review sites
- Setup completed in **under 15 minutes** for most customers

## 6. Version 2 (V2) — Growth Product

**Goal:** expand the market, make installation easier and unlock bigger customers and partner channels.

### 6.1 Microsoft 365 / Outlook Support

Bring the same experience to companies using Microsoft 365 and Outlook, including Outlook on desktop, web and mobile.

**Why:** a large share of businesses run on Microsoft, so this roughly doubles our reachable market.

### 6.2 One-Click Marketplace Install

List the product on the Google Workspace Marketplace and the Microsoft marketplace for one-click installation, after completing the required security reviews.

**Why:** makes setup effortless, builds credibility and brings in customers who search those marketplaces directly.

### 6.3 Department & Role-Based Signatures

Different signatures for different teams (Sales, Support, Leadership) or offices and countries.

**Why:** growing companies need variation while keeping the brand consistent; this is a key reason to upgrade.

### 6.4 Scheduled & Targeted Campaigns

Schedule banners to start and end automatically, and show different banners to different departments or regions.

**Why:** saves marketing time and makes campaigns more relevant.

### 6.5 Banner Testing

Run two banner versions and see which gets more clicks.

**Why:** helps marketing teams improve results and strengthens the case for the product's value.

### 6.6 Advanced Analytics & Reports

Deeper insights: clicks by department, campaign and time period, with downloadable reports.

**Why:** gives marketing teams numbers to show leadership.

### 6.7 Legal Disclaimers

Automatically add required legal or confidentiality notices, varying by country or department.

**Why:** law firms, finance companies and regulated industries need this, and they pay well.

### 6.8 Employee Self-Edit (with Approval)

Employees can update allowed fields, such as their pronouns, a personal booking link or a certification badge, while brand elements stay locked.

**Why:** gives staff some flexibility without breaking brand consistency.

### 6.9 Brand Lock & Admin Roles

Multiple admins with different permissions (for example, IT manages setup, marketing manages banners), with a history of who changed what.

**Why:** larger companies require clear control and accountability.

### 6.10 Partner Console for IT Service Providers (MSPs)

A single dashboard where an IT service provider can manage signatures for all of their client companies, with partner pricing and a reseller margin.

**Why:** one partner can bring dozens of customers, making this potentially our most efficient growth channel.

### 6.11 Integrations

Optional connections to HR and CRM tools so employee details and campaigns stay up to date.

**Why:** reduces manual work further and makes the product harder to replace.

### 6.12 Security Certification

Pursue recognized security certifications and provide ready answers to customer security questionnaires.

**Why:** required to win larger and more security-conscious customers.

### V2 Success Milestone

- Microsoft 365 customers live alongside Google Workspace customers
- Listed and installable from both marketplaces
- First **3 MSP partners** onboarded
- Steady growth from organic search and marketplace sign-ups

---

## 7. Out of Scope

To stay focused, we will **not** build:

- Email sending, email marketing or newsletter tools
- Reading, scanning or storing customers' emails
- A general-purpose design tool beyond signatures
- A custom service where customer emails pass through our systems

---

## 8. How We'll Find Customers

- **Free signature generator** to attract search traffic and convert visitors into team customers.
- **Comparison pages** for buyers already shopping for alternatives to existing tools.
- **Google Workspace and Microsoft marketplaces** where IT admins search for solutions.
- **IT service providers (MSPs)** as reseller partners.
- **Targeted outreach** to IT and marketing managers at 20–300 person companies, especially after a rebrand, funding round or product launch.
- **Software review sites** (such as G2 and Capterra) to build credibility through customer reviews.
