One-time step for each person in Gmail

In replies and forwards, Gmail normally puts a signature at the very bottom, below the earlier messages, where it is hidden behind the three dots, and adds a "--" line above it. One setting moves the signature directly under what the person writes and removes that line. Google only lets each person change it for themselves, so send your team the note below.

Hi,

Your Gmail now has our company email signature. Please do this once, so it appears directly under what you write when you reply to or forward an email:

  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon at the top right, then "See all settings".
  2. On the General tab, scroll down to "Signature".
  3. Tick "Insert signature before quoted text in replies and remove the "--" line that precedes it". Despite its name, this covers forwards too.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and click "Save changes".

It takes under a minute. Thank you!
