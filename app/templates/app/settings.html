What happens automatically

  • Every hour, FooterPilot reads your Google directory.
  • New employees get the company signature after the next reading.
  • When someone's name, job title, phone number or photo changes, their signature is updated.
  • Excluding someone on the Employees page removes the company signature from their Gmail. Including them again applies the current one.
  • People who are suspended are left alone.
