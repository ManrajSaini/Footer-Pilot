How to add it to your email

Gmail
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, open the gear icon, then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new.
  4. Paste into the box, then click Save changes at the bottom of the page.
Outlook
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. New Outlook and Outlook on the web: open Settings, then Accounts, then Signatures.
  3. Classic Outlook: open File, Options, Mail, then Signatures and click New.
  4. Paste into the editor and save.
Apple Mail
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Mail, open Settings, then Signatures, and click +.
  3. Untick Always match my default message font.
  4. Paste into the box on the right.
